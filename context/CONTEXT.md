# Context — US Trends 2027 Microsite

The product/project context: the "what" and "why." Visual identity lives in `design/DESIGN.md`.

## Summary

A microsite presenting a US point of view on two national trends for 2027, produced by Accenture Song's Design & Digital Products (D&DP) practice. The site runs in two modes: walked through live during a session with an internal Accenture designer audience, and shared afterward as an async reference artifact others can explore on their own.

## Problem

The D&DP practice needed a format to surface bottom-up US regional trend research in a way that works both as a live presentation and as a lasting reference. A deck doesn't serve the async mode; a document doesn't serve the live mode. The microsite solves both simultaneously — interactive enough to present with, self-explanatory enough to stand alone.

## Audience

Internal Accenture designers, consultants, and leaders. Assumes familiarity with design practice vocabulary. Tone is editorial and confident — not explanatory, not consultant-heavy, not bullet-point strategy.

## Goals

What success looks like.

- People explore the content — flip cards, trend narratives, regional evidence — not just skim
- The rigor of the bottom-up research methodology comes through; the site reads as a serious research artifact, not a slide deck

## Non-goals

Things that could reasonably be goals but are explicitly out of scope.

- This is not the official Accenture Life Trends report. Standard disclaimer: "inspired by but developed independently of Accenture's annual Life Trends report."
- No external publishing — internal audience only

## Constraints

- **Tone:** Editorial and confident. No em-dashes. No hedging language. No AI-sounding qualifiers.
- **Content fidelity:** Trend names are proper nouns — always "Signed by Yours Truly" and "Give Me the Receipts."
- **Two-mode design:** Must serve live presentation (walked through) and async reference (self-navigated) equally.
- **Interaction:** Flip cards and timeline tooltips are explicit format requests — not decorative choices.
- **Brand:** Accenture Song visual system. See `design/DESIGN.md` and `design/design.md`.

## Research methodology

Three-stage process:
1. **Regional evidence collection** — Five US regions (Northeast, Midwest, DC/AFS, South, West) each contributed trend observations grounded in local qualitative research, cultural observation, and behavioral signal-tracking. South contributed three; all others contributed two (eleven total).
2. **Disaggregation** — The eleven regional trends were broken into underlying signals, patterns, and tensions rather than treated as conclusions.
3. **Reassembly** — Signals recurring across enough regions to represent a genuinely national pattern were reassembled into the two national trends.

Goal: geographic breadth, honesty, and relevancy. National trends were earned from geographic breadth, not projected from coastal defaults.
